A GENTLE ROUTINE

Care for the fiber, not a guess

Synthetic fibers and cap materials differ. Check the care label before washing, drying, or styling.

✳

If something feels off, pause.

A wig should not cause pain or persistent irritation. Recheck the fit and materials; for ongoing scalp symptoms or hair loss concerns, ask a qualified health professional.

A GOOD RULE OF THUMB

“The product’s care label is the best guide to that particular wig.”

Fibers, cap construction, and heat limits are not all the same.
